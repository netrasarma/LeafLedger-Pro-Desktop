🔄 HOW LEAF LEDGER PRO WORKS

The complete commercial operational lifecycle from garden collection to factory payout

STAGE 01
Green Leaf Intake

Growers deliver freshly plucked tea leaf bags to the collection shed. The operator enters gross weight; bag tare and moisture deductions are computed instantly, and a printed slip or WhatsApp receipt is issued immediately.

STAGE 02
Planter Advances

Cash advances, fertilizer distributions, manure loans, and emergency borrowings given to growers are recorded in real-time. Running balances update automatically, preventing unrecorded or disputed loans.

STAGE 03
Factory Dispatch

Daily collected leaf is aggregated into transport trucks and dispatched to bought-leaf tea factories with official challans. Factory weighbridge weights are cross-verified against garden intake to audit transit loss.

STAGE 04
Monthly Settlement

At the close of each plucking month, the system calculates gross earnings based on applicable leaf rates, deducts amortized advances, and generates clear planter passbooks for seamless bank payments.

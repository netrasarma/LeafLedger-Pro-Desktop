# Leaf Ledger Pro

### Enterprise Tea Collection & Estate Operations Management Solution

**Leaf Ledger Pro** is an executive desktop management system engineered specifically for tea leaf agencies, collection centers, estate managers, and green leaf suppliers. It transforms manual paperwork and fragmented tally systems into a unified, high-precision digital ledger.

---

## The Solution

Managing green leaf operations involves fast-paced field collections, fluctuating garden weights, factory transit variances, and complex grower cash advances. **Leaf Ledger Pro** provides an integrated solution to handle every stage of the tea supply chain:

* **Field Intake to Factory Delivery**: Track daily intake collections directly from small growers and estates, calculate bag deductions and net weights instantly, and balance green leaf dispatches against factory delivery acceptances.
* **Grower Financial Accounting**: Manage cash advances, chemical and fertilizer disbursements, and monthly leaf settlements with automated interest and balance calculations.
* **Factory Dispatches & Transit Reconciliation**: Eliminate unexplained weight loss and disputes by reconciling estate dispatch weights against factory weighbridge receipts.
* **Offline-Ready Resilience**: Continuous operation even during rural network drops or internet outages, with automatic synchronization when connectivity is restored.

---

## Core Capabilities

### 🍃 Daily Garden Collections & Intake
* Rapid record entry for morning and evening leaf intake.
* Instant net weight computation with automated bag tare deductions and quality deductions.
* Batch ledger updates with instant thermal receipt generation.

### 💰 Advance & Financial Management
* Full financial tracking for grower advances (cash, inputs, fertilizers, and supplies).
* Automatic adjustment of chemical advances directly into grower billing cycles.
* Real-time ledger balances, grower statements, and monthly settlement sheets.

### 🏭 Factory Transit & Delivery Logs
* Complete delivery logging with transporter, vehicle number, and dispatch documentation.
* Real-time transit loss tracking (field intake weight vs. factory received weight).
* Factory payment tracking and outstanding balance monitoring.

### 📊 Business Intelligence & Reporting
* Daily, monthly, and seasonal collection summaries.
* Printable grower passbooks, billing statements, and payment vouchers.
* Thermal receipt printing and custom exportable reports.
